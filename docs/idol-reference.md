# Key Takeaways

- **Grand total:** 311 entries across three generations (before deduplication of cross-gen idols such as LOONA members appearing in ARTMS/Loossemble)
- **Most common length:** 5 letters and 6 letters are tied at 79 idols each — ideal Wordle-style targets with the deepest pools
- **4-letter pool:** 59 idols — viable for a shorter variant
- **5-letter pool:** 79 idols — strong Wordle match; canonical Wordle length
- **6-letter pool:** 79 idols — equally strong; current Idoldle v1 mode
- **7-letter pool:** 40 idols — manageable extended mode
- **8-letter pool:** 22 idols — small but functional
- **9-letter pool:** 8 idols — too small for standalone mode; usable as bonus/challenge rounds
- **Exclude from standard play:** J (STAYC, 1 letter), LE (EXID, 2 letters) — too short; Chaeryeong, Chloe Young (10 letters) — too long unless extended mode
- **Two-word stage names to decide on:** Baby Soul, Cheng Xiao, Kim Lip, Go Won, Moon Sua, Na Goeun, Chloe Young — strip spaces before counting, or house-rule them out
- **Accent-bearing names:** Rosé (→ Rose, 4 letters) is the only one in this list
